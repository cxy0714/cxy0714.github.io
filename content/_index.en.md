I'm **Xingyu Chen**, a fourth-year Ph.D. candidate in Statistics at the [School of Mathematical Sciences](https://math.sjtu.edu.cn/Default/index/), [Shanghai Jiao Tong University (SJTU)](https://www.sjtu.edu.cn/). I'm fortunate to be advised by [Professor **Lin Liu**](https://linliu-stats.github.io/).

My research interests focus on causal inference and mathematical statistics, including high-dimensional statistics, higher-order U-statistics, statistical computing, statistical-computational tradeoff, semiparametric theory, nonparametric theory, inverse problems, and efficiency theory.

Before joining SJTU, I received my B.S. in Mathematics and Applied Mathematics in 2023 from the [School of Mathematics and Statistics](https://maths.hust.edu.cn/), [Huazhong University of Science and Technology (HUST)](http://www.hust.edu.cn/).

⚡ **Fun fact:** "0714" in my ID "cxy0714" is the official subject code for **Statistics** in the Chinese Ministry of Education's classification system—where "007" denotes Science and "0701" denotes Mathematics. ([source](https://www.cdgdc.edu.cn/dslxkpgjggb/))

In February 2011, the Academic Degrees Committee of the State Council approved a reform that elevated Statistics from a sub-discipline jointly under Mathematics and Economics to an independent 一级学科 (first-level discipline), on par with its former parents. ([source](https://epaper.gmw.cn/gmrb/html/2014-02/18/nw.D1100000gmrb_20140218_1-13.htm?div=-1))
